Comparison mode

  • Two sims, one clock. SIM A (top) and SIM B (bottom) run in lockstep on the same virtual clock, each with its own charts and totals.
  • Shared traffic. The Clients knob group — count, request rate, timeouts, retries — applies to both sims, so they always see the same offered load. Speed, pause, and reset are shared too.
  • Per-sim tuning. The RTB Fabric, Downstream pools, and Downstreams groups edit one sim at a time — pick it with the SIM A / SIM B tabs in the Tuning panel.
  • Scenarios. A scenario button sets that sim's fabric & downstream tuning, and also sets the shared client settings (scenarios are designed as wholes). Storm-prone and Protected share identical client settings, so they isolate the fabric-side differences.
  • ◉ PULSE surges both sims at once — the same traffic spike, two responses.
